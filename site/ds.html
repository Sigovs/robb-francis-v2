Colour

A near-black ground with a trace of the gallery navy, cool inks, and one red sampled from the showroom door frame. No warm neutrals. Contrast is measured live against --c-ground.

Inventory groundimg/inventory-ground.jpg

A dithered radial gradient baked to an image: smoke behind the centre card, a cold bruise top left, an oxblood breath at the foot. Its last quarter lands exactly on --c-deep. CSS gradients this dark band into steps, so it stays an image.

03

Space

One scale, no magic numbers. Nothing touches by default: every element has inner padding and at least --s-3 of air to its neighbours.

Frame

04

Shape & depth

The house is square. Radius appears only where an object sits on a photograph. Hierarchy comes from tone and hairlines, not shadows.

05

Motion

Lenis carries the scroll (lerp 0.055, on the GSAP ticker). Scroll-scrubbed acts map to scroll ranges; event motion takes its duration and easing from these tokens. Under reduced motion nothing moves and every section rests in its final state.

07

Rules

  1. Nothing touches. Texts never stick together, buttons never sit flush, content never rests under the header. Every element has inner padding and at least --s-3 of air, in every pinned and animated state.
  2. The header never moves. No hide-on-scroll, no collapse. Past the hero it sits on a solid band that dissolves; no hairline under it.
  3. One page. Every nav item lands on its own section (#inventory #financing #consign #collection #about #contact). A pinned act is reached through its ScrollTrigger range, not its measured top.
  4. Voice. Proper names for cars, sentence case for leads, tracked caps only for short labels. The house phrases: By Appointment Only, Price on request, Open to Discussion, Enquire. No invented years or claims; model-level facts are marked for the client to confirm.
  5. Colour. Cool neutrals and one red from the building. Oxblood is a fill or a glow, never text. Focus is a 2px --c-oxblood-lit ring, 3px offset.
  6. Imagery. The car is the subject and the largest thing on screen. No third-party watermarks on published pages; the mockup's for-sale photos are placeholders to be replaced with the client's own.
  7. Motion. Lenis on every page; transform and opacity only; reduced motion shows every section complete and still.
